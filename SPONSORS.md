## 💖 How to Sponsor

If you or your organization would like to support BetterDesk, several options
are available:

- **GitHub Sponsors:** [github.com/sponsors/UNITRONIX](https://github.com/sponsors/UNITRONIX)
- **Buy Me a Coffee:** [buymeacoffee.com/unitronix](https://buymeacoffee.com/unitronix)
- **Code contributions:** see [CONTRIBUTING.md](docs/development/CONTRIBUTING.md)
- **Security disclosures:** see [docs/security/](docs/security/)
- **Corporate partnership / honorary supporter status:** open an issue or
  contact the maintainers via the repository.

Sponsors are acknowledged here and (with permission) in the project README.

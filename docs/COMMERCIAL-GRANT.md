# BetterDesk Sponsor Commercial Grant

Optional benefit for selected supporters. Default license: [AGPL-3.0](../LICENSE).

**Self-service** — no paperwork, no audits. GitHub Sponsors billing (or honorary
status) is the only check.

---

## In one sentence

**AGPL applies to everyone.** A **Commercial Grant** (copyleft exception for
*your private deployment patches*) applies to **Bronze Sponsor ($50/mo) and
above** on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/UNITRONIX), plus
**Honorary Supporters** — not to $5/$15 tiers or Buy Me a Coffee.

## What the grant allows

While eligible sponsorship is active, the organization may:

- Run **modified** BetterDesk (private patches, branding) for internal or
  client use,
- **Without** publishing those private modifications under AGPL,

provided they:

- Do **not** resell BetterDesk as a standalone product (OEM),
- Keep **upstream** features merged into the public repo open for everyone
  ([SPONSORS.md](../SPONSORS.md)).

**Activation:** active GitHub Sponsors subscription at **$50/month or higher**,
or Honorary Supporter status. **Ends** when sponsorship ends. No certificates.
